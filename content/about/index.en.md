Hi! I'm **Thiago Nogueira** — `thiagotn` around the web.

I've been a **software engineer** for almost two decades, based in São Paulo, Brazil. These days I
work as a **Systems Specialist / Tech Lead**, and along the way I've been through backend (Java,
Kotlin, Node.js, microservices, Spring Boot), mobile (Flutter, Android and iOS) and a good deal of
**infrastructure** — Kubernetes, CI/CD, DevOps and infrastructure as code. I enjoy writing code as
much as leading technically and helping teams grow.

Outside of work, my playground is a **homelab**: a mini PC running Kubernetes at home, where I host
personal projects and try out ideas without fear of breaking things. Most of what I write here comes
from it — Kubernetes, networking, GitOps, observability and whatever I happen to be learning at the
moment. I write in a direct, no-fluff style — the kind of content I wish I had found when I was
starting out.
